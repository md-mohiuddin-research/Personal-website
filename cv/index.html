Profile

I am a physics graduate working with Professor Alamgir Kabir at the University of Dhaka. My main experience is in computational materials science, with a focus on energy materials, semiconductors, and device-level modeling. I am actively seeking a fully funded PhD position and am also interested in materials synthesis and experimental validation.

Research interests

  • Computational optoelectronic materials, perovskites, and double perovskites.
  • Defect physics, doping control, band alignment, and nonradiative losses.
  • DFT-guided device simulation for photodiodes, photovoltaics, and sensors.
  • ML-assisted materials discovery with uncertainty-aware screening.
  • Functional nanomaterials, MOFs, heterostructures, photocatalysis, and sustainable energy solutions.
