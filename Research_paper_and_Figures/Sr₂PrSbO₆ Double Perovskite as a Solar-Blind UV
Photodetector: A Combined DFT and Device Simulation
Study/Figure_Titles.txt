Sr2PrSbO6 Solar-Blind UV-C Photodetector (HSE06 + DEVSIM TCAD, figures v3)

==========================================================================

Figure 1  (Fig_UV1_optical)
---------------------------
Title:
  Optical properties of Sr2PrSbO6 computed at the HSE06 level.

Full caption:
  Optical properties of Sr2PrSbO6 computed at the HSE06 level (VASP,
  LOPTICS). (a) Absorption coefficient alpha(lambda); the absorption edge at
  lambda_c = 244 nm (Eg = 5.09 eV) lies inside the solar-blind UV-C band
  (200-280 nm), and alpha = 0 for lambda > lambda_c. (b) Film absorption
  efficiency eta_abs = (1-R)(1-exp(-alpha*d)) for film thicknesses of
  200 nm - 5 um. (c) Refractive index n(lambda) and extinction coefficient
  k(lambda). (d) Real (eps_1) and imaginary (eps_2) parts of the dielectric
  function; the static value eps_1(0) = 3.47.

Figure 2  (Fig_UV2_device_band)
-------------------------------
Title:
  Device architecture and equilibrium band diagram of the
  Pt/Sr2PrSbO6/Pt MSM photodetector.

Full caption:
  (a) Schematic of the metal-semiconductor-metal (MSM) photoconductor:
  a Sr2PrSbO6 absorber (d = 200 nm - 5 um) between Pt electrodes
  (L = 10 um, W = 100 um, A = 1e-4 cm^2) under UV-C illumination
  (h*nu > 5.09 eV) and an applied field of 10 kV/cm at V = 10 V.
  (b) Equilibrium band diagram (V = 0) with back-to-back Schottky
  barriers Phi_B = 2.15 eV at the Pt contacts (HSE06: Eg = 5.09 eV,
  eps_r = 3.47, chi = 3.5 eV).

Figure 3  (Fig_UV3_IV)
----------------------
Title:
  Simulated dark and illuminated current-voltage characteristics of the
  Sr2PrSbO6 MSM photodetector.

Full caption:
  Dark and UV-illuminated (220 nm, 1 mW/cm^2) I-V characteristics from
  DEVSIM TCAD drift-diffusion simulation at T = 300 K. The photocurrent
  exceeds the intrinsic dark current by ~13 orders of magnitude
  (I_ph/I_dark ~ 5e13 at 10 V), corresponding to R(220 nm) = 2.7 mA/W
  for the 200 nm baseline device.

Figure 4  (Fig_UV4_FEM_responsivity)
------------------------------------
Title:
  Spatial device profiles and spectral responsivity of the Sr2PrSbO6 MSM
  photodetector.

Full caption:
  (a) Electric-field deviation |E| - E0 (E0 = 10 kV/cm) across the
  electrode gap in the dark and under 220 nm illumination at V = 10 V.
  (b) Electron and hole density profiles in the dark and under
  illumination. (c) Beer-Lambert optical generation profile through the
  film depth at 220 nm (penetration depth 1/alpha = 0.21 um).
  (d-f) Spectral responsivity R(lambda) as a function of (d) bias voltage
  (5-20 V), (e) film thickness (200 nm - 5 um), and (f) carrier lifetime
  (0.1-10 ns); the thick curve marks the baseline device (V = 10 V,
  d = 200 nm, tau = 1 ns). Responsivity vanishes above lambda_c = 244 nm,
  confirming intrinsic solar-blind operation.

Figure 5  (Fig_UV5_performance)
-------------------------------
Title:
  Simulated performance metrics of the Sr2PrSbO6 solar-blind UV-C
  photodetector.

Full caption:
  (a) External quantum efficiency, EQE(220 nm) = 1.53% (V = 10 V,
  d = 200 nm). (b) Specific detectivity D*(220 nm) = 1.5e9 Jones using a
  literature dark-current density of 1e-10 A/cm^2. (c) Responsivity at key
  wavelengths: finite response for lambda < 244 nm and zero (alpha = 0)
  response beyond the cutoff, demonstrating full solar-blind rejection.
  (d) Responsivity versus film thickness at 220 nm and 200 nm.
  (e) Transient photoresponse with tau = 1 ns, giving t90 rise and fall
  times of 2.3 ns. (f) Photocurrent linearity over five decades of
  incident power (slope = 1.000). (g, h) Sensitivity maps of responsivity
  and log10(D*) versus film thickness and carrier lifetime; the star
  marks the baseline device.

Figure 6  (Fig_UV6_literature)
------------------------------
Title:
  Benchmarking of the predicted Sr2PrSbO6 photodetector against reported
  solar-blind UV detectors.

Full caption:
  Responsivity at 220 nm (V = 10 V) versus band gap for reported
  solar-blind photodetectors (beta-Ga2O3, AlGaN, MgZnO, Ga2O3 nanowires,
  BaSnO3, diamond) and this work (stars: 5 um and 200 nm Sr2PrSbO6
  films). The shaded region marks the solar-blind band-gap range
  (Eg > 4.4 eV); the dashed line traces the experimental frontier.
