Conformal prediction quantifies the reliability limits of machine-learned bandgap screening

First authorMaterials informaticsUncertainty-aware MLOxide double perovskites

Why it matters. A fast prediction is only useful when its uncertainty is visible. This project studies how conformal prediction can turn machine-learned bandgap screening into a more honest prioritization tool for expensive DFT calculations and eventual experiments.

The research question

When a model screens a large crystal dataset, how can we tell which predictions are reliable enough to guide the next calculation or synthesis step?

Working approach

  • Build structural and compositional descriptors with pymatgen and Materials Project data.
  • Compare supervised learners including linear models, KNN, and XGBoost.
  • Use conformal prediction to attach empirical reliability bounds to bandgap estimates.
  • Prioritize candidates by both predicted property and uncertainty rather than by a point estimate alone.

Where it fits

This work extends my computational materials practice from individual compounds toward search. The aim is a workflow that helps decide where a calculation or experiment is worth the time, while keeping the reliability limits visible to the researcher.
