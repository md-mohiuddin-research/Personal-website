My research sits at the intersection of computational materials science, semiconductor devices, and experimental materials chemistry. I am interested in energy and environmentally responsible technologies where better understanding at the atomic scale can change what is possible at the device scale.

Working style

I care about transparent assumptions, reproducible workflows, and results that survive contact with a real device. In practice that means checking stability before optimization, tracing parameter provenance, visualizing the result clearly, and writing the reasoning so that another researcher can follow it.
