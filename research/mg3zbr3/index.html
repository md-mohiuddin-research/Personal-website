Stability analysis and optoelectronic properties of Mg₃ZBr₃ perovskites for PIN photodiodes

First authorDFT + HSE06Phonons + device simulationLead-free halide perovskites
Read the paper ↗Open DOI ↗

Why it matters. Lead-free Mg₃ZBr₃ (Z = As, Sb, Bi) offers a tunable platform for photodetection without relying on lead chemistry. The study combines structural, phonon, electronic, optical, mechanical, and PIN-device analysis to understand how the pnictogen controls performance.

The research question

Can a chemically simple, lead-free perovskite family provide a controllable path from lattice stability to useful photodiode response? We compared the As, Sb, and Bi compositions across the full structure–property pipeline.

What I did

  • Performed first-principles calculations and HSE06 band-structure analysis for the three compositions.
  • Evaluated phonon dispersion, elastic stability, optical functions, and transport trends.
  • Linked the calculated band edges and optical response to drift–diffusion PIN photodiode simulations.
  • Handled data processing, plotting, interpretation, and the original draft as a joint first author.
2.0645eV HSE06 gap for Mg₃AsBr₃
1.6533eV HSE06 gap for Mg₃SbBr₃
1.5226eV HSE06 gap for Mg₃BiBr₃

From bandgap to device response

The calculated trend down the pnictogen series narrows the bandgap and shifts the spectral response toward longer wavelengths. The simulated PIN devices reflect that shift: composition becomes a design parameter for selecting the photodiode cut-on window.

Citation: M. Mohiuddin, M. M. Hasan, and A. Kabir, “Stability Analysis and Optoelectronic Properties of Mg₃ZBr₃ (Z = As, Sb, Bi) Perovskites for Evaluating the Performance in PIN Photodiodes,” ACS Omega 11 (2026), 12117–12137. DOI: 10.1021/acsomega.5c11440.
